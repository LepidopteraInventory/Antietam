Antietam Lepidoptera

Overview

Grassland restoration has been an important activity supporting native biodiversity at National Parks in the Eastern US. At Antietam National Battlefield in Sharpsburg, MD, restoration work is ongoing near Miller Farm and Otto Farm.

To understand how these grasslands support native biodiversity, this Inventory will document what species of Lepidoptera (moths and butterflies) utilize these restored grasslands. The Inventory is being led by Elise Larsen at Georgetown University, and is scheduled for 2026-2028.

We will use photography to document individual moths and butterflies found in and adjacent to the grassland restoration projects at Antietam National Battlefield. Documentation of night-flying moths will begin in late 2026 and continue through 2027. Field crews will actively survey day-flying moths and butterflies in 2027. We expect this inventory project to be completed by June 2028.
